# Mission Reflection

During this laboratory activity, I learned more about the differences and similarities of AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. At first, I thought that cloud providers were almost the same because they all provide services for computing, storage, networking, and security. However, after researching each platform, I realized that each provider has its own strengths and is designed to support different business needs.

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is AWS because of its wide range of services and flexibility. I learned that AWS can support different types of organizations, from small startups to large enterprises. Azure also impressed me because of its strong integration with Microsoft technologies such as Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. Meanwhile, Google Cloud stood out to me because of its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, security, and scalable cloud infrastructure. They also allow organizations to access computing resources without needing to maintain all physical hardware themselves.

I also learned that business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. A company should consider its budget, existing technologies, location, workload, scalability, security, and future plans. There is no single cloud provider that is always the best for every organization.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing cloud services is not only about technical features. I also need to understand what the client actually needs before recommending a solution.

Finally, this mission improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio because I added a complete multi-cloud research project. It also helped me practice documentation, comparison, decision-making, Linux investigation, and presenting technical information in a more organized way.
